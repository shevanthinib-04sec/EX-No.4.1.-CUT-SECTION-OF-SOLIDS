# EX No.4.1. CUT SECTION OF SOLIDS
## DATE:

## AIM
To create the solid model from given drawing and section the solid model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3.16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:
1.Select the XY Plane and start a new sketch.
2.Draw the base profile using two end circles of R15 (Ø30 mm) and connect them with tangent lines to form the flange shape.


![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825274-c6a8d3ee-fb19-4641-b915-8d2e000c40a8.png)

3.Sketch two holes of Ø12 mm at the centers of the end circles as given in the drawing.
4.Draw the central concentric circles of Ø50 mm and Ø30 mm at the midpoint of the flange.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825282-0d3de4b8-b6d9-446a-ae2a-461955057399.png)

5.Apply all the required dimensions and constraints according to the given drawing.
6.Use the Trim and other sketch modification tools to remove unwanted portions and complete the sketch.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825301-194365b4-69d2-42ad-8545-a68d3ca1f442.png)

7.Click Finish Sketch to finalize the 2D profile.
8.Use the Extrude command and extrude the flange profile to a thickness of 10 mm.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825314-b90f6aba-da26-4f0d-8d61-a03e8ad0d765.png)

9.Select the central Ø50 mm circle and extrude it upward by 28 mm to create the cylindrical boss.
10.Use the Hole or Extrude Cut feature to create the central Ø20 mm through hole.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825328-205fac21-1322-4d67-894b-6fd92e454e26.png)

11.Create the counterbore of Ø30 mm and 6 mm depth at the top of the boss.
12.Create the side hole of Ø6 mm through the cylindrical boss as shown in the drawing.


![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825337-455049de-f053-48b8-9dd0-ebeb189c0dde.png)

13.Use the Inspect → Section Analysis command, select the center plane, and generate the cut section view of the solid model.
14.Save the model and verify that the dimensions match the given sectional drawing.
